Overview

One of the most important things we do in this program is to help give you a clear-eyed assessment of who you truly are. We all have patterns, behaviors, traits and experiences that define how we show up to ourselves and to others. Understanding this about ourselves is critical if you are to become an authentic, empathetic and audacious leader.

A mirror and a compass

We say this report is both a mirror and a compass. The mirror is reflecting back to you what the psychometric assessments say about you. The compass part is more about interpreting what these assessments say about who you are, plus who you should become and where you should go in your career and life. The mirror is your starting point. The compass points toward the direction you should head.

The mirror reflects who you are now; the compass points where to go. The report is both.

The five assessments

In the Foundations Report, we will review several psychometric reports about you. These assessments are meant to help you see who you really are. Do not try to trick these assessments, rather answer them in a calm, relaxed state as close to your true self as you can be.

  • Myers-Briggs (MBTI): check your email for the invite to take the assessment. If you do not see an email (check your junk/spam folders), email support@vmleader.com and we will send you an invite directly.
  • DISC assessment: complete it via the DISC assessment link provided.
  • Leadership DNA: complete it via the Leadership DNA link provided.
  • CliftonStrengths: check your email for the invite. Note: if you have taken CliftonStrengths before (even years ago), you will get an error using the emailed code, they only allow you to take it once. In that case, log in to your account, download your previous report, and send it to us.
  • Culture Index: this report takes a few days to reach us after you complete it, so allow a little processing time.
If you joined Vertical Motion before April 2025, you did not have to complete these assessments. This applies only to members who joined April 1, 2025 or later.

Self SWOT analysis

Alongside the psychometrics, complete a Self SWOT analysis, an honest look at your own strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. Use the worksheet, and send your completed SWOT to support@vmleader.com.

An honest self-inventory that sits alongside the psychometric data.

Book your Foundations Call

Once you've completed and submitted the assessments, it's time to book your Foundations Call, this is the live session where we walk you through your results together and turn the mirror into a compass. We will send you a booking link once we confirm we have all of your completed assessments. Because the Culture Index report takes a few days to reach us, please allow a little time to confirm receipt and send your link. If it has been 4 business days since you submitted your assessments and you haven't heard from us, email support@vmleader.com.

Why this matters: everything downstream, your Zone of Value, your dial, your brand, how you interview and lead, is sharper when it's built on a true picture of who you are. The Foundations Report is where that picture comes from. Do this early, and do it honestly.
