First, the mental model: Cowork vs Claude Code

Think of Cowork as your AI employee and Claude Code as your AI engineer. You'll live in Cowork and only reach for Code when you're building an actual software tool. Both are the same Claude underneath. What differs is the workspace and what it can build.

CoworkClaude Code
What it isAn AI teammate that works your files, apps, and browserAn AI developer that writes and runs real code
Best forRunning the business day to day: docs, dashboards, trackers, research, admin, simple agentsBuilding custom software: apps, integrations, automations with real logic
How you use itPlain conversation. You describe the outcome and watch it actPlain conversation too, but it produces and runs a codebase
Skill neededNoneComfort reviewing what it builds. Still no hand-coding
Rule of thumb: Start every task in Cowork. Move to Claude Code only when you need a durable, reusable tool you'll run again and again, and even then, Claude writes the code, not you. Most solopreneurs run their first year almost entirely in Cowork.

Build it: follow along in Cowork

Do each step in order. Copy the prompt, run it, and adjust by conversation ("make it simpler," "add a column") until it's right.

  1. Load your knowledge. Connect Cowork to your CFA folder, then prove it worked.
    Here is my CFA course folder and my completed worksheets. Read them and tell me: my offer, my current stage, and my top 3 gaps.
  2. Build your dashboard. Turn the course into a home base you reopen anytime.
    Build me a one-page business dashboard I can reopen and update. Sections: my CFA stage progress (the 6 stages), this week's KPIs, active deals, and my top 3 tasks.
  3. Build your KPI tracker.
    Create a weekly KPI tracker for a solo founder in the CFA: validation conversations, sales calls booked, offers made, revenue, and LinkedIn activity. Make it a table I update by telling you the numbers.
  4. Build your dealflow tracker.
    Create a dealflow tracker: each prospect with stage (Lead, Conversation, Proposal, Won or Lost), value, next action, and date. Let me add deals just by chatting.
  5. Add a light cash view.
    Add a simple cash view: monthly revenue, expenses, and runway in months.
  6. Update by conversation. This is the magic moment. You never touch a spreadsheet again.
    Add a deal: Acme Co, proposal stage, $6k, follow up Friday. Also, this week I had 5 validation calls and made 2 offers, update my KPIs.
  7. Hire your first agent. Turn a weekly chore into an automation.
    Every Monday at 7am, review my trackers and send me a digest: last week's KPIs, which deals moved, and the 3 things that need me this week.
  8. Know where Claude Code comes in (nothing to build yet). When you outgrow this, a calculator your clients use, or your Stripe revenue flowing in automatically, that's a Claude Code job. Same conversation, but Claude builds real software you own.
End state: a working dashboard, three live trackers, and one agent, all built by talking.

Give your AI the full picture of you

The more your AI knows about you, the better it coaches you, on the course, your career, and your business. Beyond the CFA files, load two more things:

  • Your LDNA assessment. Share your results and ask Claude to name your complementary archetypes and your blind spots, then have it deliberately play those roles for you, covering the gaps a co-founder normally would.
  • Your LinkedIn. Give Claude your profile so it knows your background, positioning, and network.

Two ways to load them: turn on Claude in Chrome (or let Claude drive your computer) and point it at the page, or just copy and paste. Either works. Once Claude has your CFA work, your LDNA, and your LinkedIn, it stops being a generic assistant and becomes a coach that actually knows you.

Your operating lens: Eliminate, Automate, Delegate

Run every recurring task through one question, in this order:

  1. Eliminate. Does this need doing at all? The cheapest task is the one you kill.
  2. Automate. If it must happen and it's repeatable, hand it to an agent or scheduled task. No human touch.
  3. Delegate. If it needs judgment, delegate it: first to AI with your review, later to a person.

Example, a weekly client update: Eliminate (does it need to be weekly?), then Automate (an agent drafts it from your trackers every Friday), then Delegate (you glance, approve, send). One chore, cut down and mostly handed off.

Your staff of agents: what to hand off

You're building a staff of agents, each one a job title AI holds. Set each up as a one-off (ask when you need it), a scheduled task (runs on a clock without you), or a saved agent (a specialist you invoke on demand).

FunctionJobs you can hand to an agent
ResearchProspect and company briefs, market and competitor scans, a daily news summary
MarketingDraft posts from your wins, turn one idea into a week of content, a content calendar
SalesPre-call briefs, post-call follow-ups, nudges on stalled deals, proposal drafts
DeliveryOnboarding packets, meeting notes with action items, first-draft deliverables
Finance & adminWeekly KPI and cash digest, invoice reminders, expense sorting, inbox triage
Personal opsA morning plan from your calendar, an end-of-day review, a weekly "what needs me" digest

The same pattern works across every major assistant (as of September 2026): Claude Cowork is your home base, with no-code Scheduled Tasks that run in the cloud; Claude Code adds routines on a schedule, webhook, or GitHub event; ChatGPT Work mode connects to Slack, Gmail, Drive, and Salesforce and runs on a schedule; Google Gemini has Scheduled Actions and an Agent Mode for Workspace; Microsoft Copilot runs agents across Microsoft 365. Learn the pattern, not the buttons.

The principle: add an agent before you add a person. Your org chart is a list of agents. You only hire a human when one is genuinely cheaper or better than the agent doing that job.

Do this now: your setup checklist

Set up

  • Confirm your Claude account has Cowork enabled
  • Put all your CFA worksheets in one folder
  • Open Cowork and connect that folder

Build

  • Load knowledge: have Claude summarize your offer, stage, and top gaps
  • Build your business dashboard
  • Build your KPI, dealflow, and cash trackers
  • Practice a conversational update: add one deal and log this week's KPIs

Automate & commit

  • Build agent #1: your Monday metrics digest
  • Pick two more jobs from the catalog above and build them
  • Book a recurring 15-minute Friday slot to update your trackers

A few ground rules

  • Keep yourself in the loop for money and sending. Review anything before it goes to a client or moves funds.
  • Own your accounts and data, and keep a copy of your key trackers.
  • Never paste passwords or secrets into a prompt.
Keep going. Add an agent before you add a person. After every worksheet you complete, tell Claude "add this to my dashboard." By the end of the CFA, your operating system runs your company.
