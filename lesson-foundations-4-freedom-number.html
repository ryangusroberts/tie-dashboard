Your Freedom Number

Runway, walkaway, and what "enough" really is, so every move you make is a decision, not a fear.

Freedom looks different for everyone

There are three levels of freedom to unlock: Level 1, Financial Freedom: you have enough money to meet your lifestyle goals. Level 2, Personal Freedom: you have enough time to do what you want. Level 3, Purpose Freedom: you have the resources to live fully in your life purpose. Today we focus on Level 1, because you can't build on a cracked foundation.

The number-one reason careers and businesses stall isn't a bad plan. It's running out of money, or the fear of it, before the plan works. Cash and runway buy you the calm to make good decisions.

Why this comes first

Before you chase a promotion, run a search, or land a client, you need to know one thing: how long can I survive while I figure this out? That's your runway. At the end of it is your walkaway number, the income threshold that lets you leave a bad situation, or corporate altogether, without gambling your family's security.

The runway and walkaway calculator

Part 1, current position: your real monthly living expenses (not aspirational), current liquid reserves, additional income sources (spouse, investments, side income), and your monthly shortfall if income stopped today.

Part 2, your runway: the total months you can operate without matching your current income, your "red zone" (when you MUST be generating revenue) and your "comfort zone" (when you'd prefer to be).

Part 3, your walkaway number: the minimum monthly income to leave, the freedom number that replaces your lifestyle (not just your bills), and the bigger freedom number that gives you options you don't even need yet.

Your walkaway number is NOT your current salary. It's your minimum viable lifestyle income plus a buffer, usually 60 to 80% of current take-home.

The math of runway

In the example above, 11 months is how long you have to build something that generates $9,000/month before you're in trouble.

The psychology of the number

Knowing your runway creates three things. Clarity: you stop making fear-based decisions and start making strategic ones. Urgency: not panic, urgency. Urgency focuses you; panic scatters you. A target: you're not building "a career" or "a business," you're generating $X by Month Y. That's specific, measurable, and achievable.

Your Freedom Number also sets the dial. A short runway leans you toward Career or Bridge for now; a longer one opens Build. Either way, you decide from the math, not the fear.

Your worksheet. Work the Runway & Walkaway calculator in your dashboard and record four things: your monthly burn rate, your runway in months, your walkaway (freedom) number, and your target date for hitting it.

If you leave this lesson without knowing your runway and your freedom number, you're building on quicksand.
